📜 The Viking Age Timeline

Roughly AD 793 to 1066: three centuries that reshaped Europe.

Lindisfarne

AD 793

Norse raiders hit the monastery at Lindisfarne in England. The shock across Christian Europe marks the traditional start of the Viking Age.

Dublin founded

AD 841

Vikings set up a fortified base, the beginning of the city of Dublin.

The Great Heathen Army

AD 865

A huge Viking force invades England and conquers most of its kingdoms. Only Alfred the Great's Wessex holds out.

Iceland settled

c. AD 874

Norse settlers arrive in Iceland. In 930 they found the Althing, one of the oldest parliaments in the world.

Normandy

AD 911

The Viking leader Rollo is granted land in France. His descendants, the Normans, later conquer England.

Vinland

c. AD 1000

Leif Erikson reaches North America, nearly 500 years before Columbus. The site at L'Anse aux Meadows, Newfoundland, proves it.

Stamford Bridge

AD 1066

Harald Hardrada dies invading England. His defeat traditionally marks the end of the Viking Age.

🙅 Myth vs. Fact

Pop culture loves Vikings, but it gets plenty wrong.

Myth

Vikings wore horned helmets.

Fact

No Viking-Age horned helmet has ever been found. The look comes from 1800s costume design, especially for Wagner's operas.

Myth

"Viking" was the name of a people.

Fact

"Going a-viking" meant going on an overseas raid. Most Norse people were farmers who never raided at all.

Myth

Vikings were filthy and unkempt.

Fact

Archaeologists find tons of combs, tweezers, and ear spoons. English writers even complained Vikings bathed every Saturday.

Myth

Vikings drank from skulls.

Fact

That's a mistranslation of an old poem. They drank from horns and cups.

Myth

Only men were warriors.

Fact

DNA showed a famous warrior grave at Birka, Sweden, belonged to a woman, buried with weapons and a strategy game.

Myth

Vikings only raided.

Fact

They traded as far as Baghdad. Thousands of Arabic silver coins have been found buried in Scandinavia.
