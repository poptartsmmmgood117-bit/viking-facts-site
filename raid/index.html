⛵ Longship Raid

It is 793 CE. The raiding season is short. Sail your longship along the coast, plunder monasteries and towns, dodge the Saxon patrols, and bring the silver home to the fjord before winter closes the seas.

  • Steer: ←→ or AD   Row: ↑/W   Slow: ↓/S
  • Row hard: hold Space (uses crew stamina)
  • Fight back: F or J fires a volley of arrows at the nearest enemy (on phones, tap 🏹). Three hits sink a Saxon ship, and ramming one at full speed hurts it more than you.
  • Sunk ships drop silver chests. Sail over them to grab the silver.
  • Know your enemy: grey patrols ram, green archer ships keep their distance and shoot, and a purple flagship is slow, tough and very rich.
  • Shipwright: at the home fjord press E (or tap 🛠) to spend silver on a tougher hull, a bigger hold, faster archers, more rowers and fire arrows (G or 🔥), which set ships burning. Spending never lowers your score.
  • Weather: fog hides you from patrols (and them from you). Storms push your ship with the wind.
  • Touch: hold a finger on the sea and the ship heads there
  • Slow down next to a town to raid it. Its archers will shoot back, and the raid alerts nearby patrols.
  • Sail back to your home fjord 🏠 to bank silver and repair. Sinking loses your cargo.
  • Sailing with the wind (the arrow at top right) is faster.
